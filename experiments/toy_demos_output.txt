  league pmf [0.5  0.03 0.31 0.15 0.01], mean 1.140 ppp, H = 1.653 bits/possession
  I(outcome; offense only (sd 0.035 ppp)     ) = 0.58 millibits/possession

  I(outcome; possession start type)   = 3.07 millibits/possession
  -> nearly all of the ~1.6 bits are irreducible pre-possession noise;
     model comparisons live in the 3rd-4th decimal place of bits/possession.

E. Apparent hot hand from a game-level latent; Miller-Sanjurjo bias
  iid shots, p=0.5                : pooled P(make|make)-P(make|miss) = +0.0010; per-game average = -0.0516
  game-level latent sd=0.3 logit  : pooled P(make|make)-P(make|miss) = +0.0188; per-game average = -0.0560
  -> pooled estimates confound between-game heterogeneity with momentum;
     per-game averages are biased negative even for iid shots.
